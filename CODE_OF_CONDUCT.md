# Code of Conduct

RenderClaw aims to be a welcoming open source project.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior:

- being respectful and constructive;
- giving useful feedback;
- accepting different experience levels;
- focusing criticism on ideas and code, not people;
- helping keep the project safe and honest.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, threats, or personal attacks;
- discriminatory language or behavior;
- publishing private information;
- spam or bad-faith disruption;
- encouraging deceptive SEO or crawler abuse.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block users when behavior harms the community or project.

If you see a problem, contact the maintainers privately.
